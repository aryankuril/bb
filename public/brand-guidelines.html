06 ICONOGRAPHY LINE FIRST · 2PX STROKE · YELLOW ACCENT

LINES, NOT DECORATIONS.

A consistent line-icon system. 2px stroke, 24px frame, rounded line caps. Yellow as the accent dot — never as the full fill. One yellow detail per icon, max.

One yellow accent per icon, never two. Stroke only, no filled illustrations. Use the icon system as visual punctuation — every section can hold one icon, never a wall of them. For UI controls (buttons, nav), prefer text labels over icon-only.

Off-centre subject. Strong negative space. Foreground anchor + mid-ground subject. Avoid flat eye-level "team photos".

08 VOICE & TONE BOMBAY DIRECT · NO FLUFF

SAY IT SHARP.
SAY IT ONCE.

We write the way Mumbai talks — quickly, clearly, with one good idea per sentence. We don't pad. We don't moralise. We don't open emails with "I hope this finds you well". We respect the reader's clock.

WE DO.

  • Open with the point. The sentence after the headline should already be the answer.
  • Name the work. "We grew their organic by 4× in 90 days." Not "We helped them on their journey".
  • Use Mumbai when it fits. Local words land harder than borrowed ones.
  • Show the receipts. Numbers, screenshots, before/after. Always.
  • Punch up. Take real positions. Disagree publicly when we mean it.

WE DON'T.

  • Use "synergy", "leverage", "ecosystem", "thought leader", "delve into", or any LinkedIn auto-suggest.
  • Write 4 paragraphs when 2 lines are honest.
  • Hide bad news in passive voice. "A delay was experienced" → "We slipped two days. Here's why."
  • Use emojis as filler. The yellow already does the smiling.
  • Sub-tweet competitors or moralise from a stage.
We turned down a ₹40L retainer last month.

The brief was clear, the budget was real, and the founder was a good guy. We still said no — because the deliverables were a 12-month treadmill of "more posts, more reels, more more". No outcome, no thesis, no exit.

Bombay Blokes doesn't do treadmills. We do work that ends. If your agency can't tell you when the engagement is done, you don't have an agency. You have a subscription.

14 MOTION PRINCIPLES WEB · SOCIAL · DECK

MOTION WITH PURPOSE.

Motion in Bombay Blokes' work is functional, not decorative. It directs attention, communicates state, and suggests speed. Never seasonal, never trendy, never "for the showreel".

// 01 — CRISP
200–300ms transitions.
Buttons, hovers, modals. No 600ms easing curves. We don't have time and neither does the user.
// 02 — DIRECTIONAL
Movement implies meaning.
Forward = progress. Up = reveal. Down = dismiss. Never random parallax for decoration.
// 03 — YELLOW LEADS
The accent moves first.
In stat reveals or interactive elements, the yellow element animates in 100ms before the supporting text.
// 04 — STILLNESS WINS
Most things shouldn't move.
Hero copy stays still. Body text stays still. If it doesn't add meaning, it doesn't get an animation.
15 BRAND SUMMARY DO'S & DON'TS

PIN THIS ABOVE
THE DESK.

Everything in this document, condensed.

DO.

  • Lead with display type. Make the headline the hero.
  • Use yellow as the spike — never as the floor.
  • Honour the bulb-O. It's the brand's idea.
  • Show the work. Numbers, screenshots, before/after.
  • Write the way Mumbai talks: short, sharp, direct.
  • Trust the 8pt grid and the 12-col layout.
  • Use mono labels for system + structure.
  • Keep one idea per asset. Always.

DON'T.

  • Restyle the wordmark. The mark is fixed.
  • Flood backgrounds with yellow. The spark needs the dark.
  • Use gradients, drop shadows, glows, or 3D effects.
  • Use stocky agency clichés (laptop + coffee, fake teamwork shots).
  • Add a second display font. Archivo Black holds it down.
  • Use emoji as filler or "decoration". The yellow does that job.
  • Write LinkedIn-speak: "synergy", "leverage", "ecosystem", "delve".
  • Ship a deck longer than 18 slides.
